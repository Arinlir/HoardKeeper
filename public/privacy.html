Privacy policy

Binderwyrm (formerly HoardKeeper): the website, the Android and iPhone app, and the server at hoard.arinvault.eu.
Effective 28 September 2026.

The short version. Binderwyrm keeps your Magic: The Gathering collection either on your phone only or on a Binderwyrm server you choose. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and nothing is sold. Card scanning happens on your device, and photos never leave it. To show cards and prices, your device talks directly to Scryfall and to a currency-rate service, which see your IP address the way any website does.

1. Who is responsible

Binderwyrm and the server at hoard.arinvault.eu are made and run by Michael Ondráček, Prague, Czech Republic (“we”). Contact: privacy@arinvault.eu.

Binderwyrm is open source and anyone can run their own server. If you connect the app to someone else's server, that person runs it and is responsible for the data stored on it. This policy covers the app itself and our own server.

2. What is stored, and where

Your collection (“vault”)

Your cards, collections, what you paid, prices, decks, wishlist, game records and display settings. Where it's kept is your choice:

  • This phone / this browser only: kept in the app's storage on your device. Nobody else, including us, has a copy. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it, so use Export to keep a backup.
  • On a Binderwyrm server: kept on that server in your account, so it follows you between devices.

Email and password

A server stores your email address and a hash of your password, and keeps you signed in with a session. Your email is used only to sign you in and to send account emails (confirmation, password reset). You can delete the account in Settings.

  • An optional public handle is the name in your shared links and in the playgroup list. It is visible to everyone who is signed in to that server, and anyone can open the pages it unlocks (your wishlist, and your collection only if you switch that on). With no handle, nothing is public.
  • Servers that used to run PIN profiles keep the old profile files, read-only, until their owners move them into an account. Moving one copies it into the account and leaves the old file as a backup.

What the app keeps on your device

  • The server address you connected to (phone app).
  • A sign-in session.
  • Display preferences.
  • The Life Counter's current game.
  • Cached card-set lists, so it's faster next time.

3. The camera

The camera is used only while you are scanning a card, and only after you allow it:

  • In the phone app, text is read on the device (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iPhone).
  • On the website, a photo you take or upload is read in your browser.
  • In both cases the only thing used afterwards is the set code and collector number. Images are not uploaded, not stored, and not sent to us or anyone else.

4. Services your device contacts directly

To show cards, prices and currency conversions, the app fetches public data from these services. Like any website, they see your IP address and the request (for example, which card was looked up). They receive no collection data, PIN, email or name from us.

ServiceWhy
Scryfall (api.scryfall.com, cards.scryfall.io) Card details, card images, market prices, set lists.
Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.app), falling back to ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) USD → EUR / CZK exchange rates, at most about once a day.
jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) Website only: the first time you scan a card on the website, the text-recognition language file is downloaded from here. The phone app doesn't use it.

Links to TCGplayer, Cardmarket and Ko-fi only take you to those sites when you tap them. After that, their own privacy policies apply. Our server also downloads Scryfall's public card database once a day. That download contains no information about you.

5. What you choose to share

  • Wishlist link (/w/<profile>): anyone with the address can see your wishlist, plus cards you've marked as wanting a replacement. Nothing else is shown.
  • Read-only guest view (/v/<profile>): off unless you turn it on in Settings. It shows cards, prices and decks, never what you paid.
  • Playgroup trades: off unless you turn it on. Other profiles on the same server can then see which of your cards match their wishlists.

6. Security records on our server

  • To stop people guessing passwords, the server remembers the IP addresses that entered a wrong password. This is kept in memory only, for at most one hour, and never written to disk.
  • The reverse proxy in front of the server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, address requested, browser type) to keep the service secure and working. They are deleted after 30 days.

7. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Storing and syncing your vault, and your account: needed to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Security records: our legitimate interest in keeping the service and your data safe (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Camera: used only when you start a scan and your device's permission allows it.

8. How long data is kept

  • Your vault stays on the server until you delete it (Settings → Account → Delete). Deletion removes it immediately.
  • Server backups that contain it are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Data kept only on your device is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you. You can also object to or restrict how it's used. Most of this you can do yourself in the app:

  • Export gives you your whole collection as a file.
  • Settings lets you rename or delete your profile or account.

For anything else, write to privacy@arinvault.eu. You can also complain to a data protection authority. In the Czech Republic that is the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (Office for Personal Data Protection).

10. Children

Binderwyrm is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly store data about them. If you believe a child has created a profile on our server, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
